Our story

A little French house, named for Lafayette.

Môtier takes its name from Gilbert du Motier, the Marquis de Lafayette, the Frenchman who crossed the Atlantic to stand with America. Chef Ivar Setiawan made a similar crossing: born in Indonesia, raised in France, and trained in its kitchens, he came to Clifton to cook in the town his mother called home.

The heart of the house is viennoiserie: croissants, pains au chocolat, and canelés baked each morning. At lunch, a short menu is written on the chalkboard by the door and changes with the market. On Friday nights, the room becomes a small dinner service with a prix fixe menu.
